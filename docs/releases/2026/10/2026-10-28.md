Clarified the current ES 8.8 Cloud Connected SOC workforce prerequisites after
the official 8.8 agent guide changed following the October 27 watch. ES 8.8 CMP
use requires Platform 10.4+, ES 8.8+ and a separate Cloud Connect 2.0 download.

VersionCompass presents that only as a dependency-packaging statement, not as
evidence that ES 8.9 has been released, dated, rolled out, entitled, regionally
available, authorized or paired with SOAR.

SOC Analyst CMP paths in ES Editions and AI Availability. Cloud-only agents stay
Cloud only. The Connector Builder and Triage conflicts, Guided Response
enhancement-scope ambiguity, AI SOC Analyst task limits and exact App for SOAR /
Export matrices remain unchanged.
